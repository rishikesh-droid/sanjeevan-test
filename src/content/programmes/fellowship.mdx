## About the fellowships

Sanjeevan Medical Foundation's **Dr. D. K. Gosavi Memorial Shri Siddhivinayak Ganapati
Cancer Hospital, Miraj** offers one-year fellowship courses, affiliated to the
**Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS), Nashik**, in two subjects:

- **Onco-Pathology** — 1 year
- **Head & Neck Cancer Surgery** — 1 year

## Courses & seats

| Fellowship Course | Duration | Seats | Total Fees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Onco-Pathology | 1 Year (1 Aug – 31 Jul) | 1 | ₹ 1,00,000 |
| Head & Neck Cancer Surgery | 1 Year (1 Aug – 31 Jul) | 2 | ₹ 1,00,000 |

## Eligibility

- **Onco-Pathology** — MD (Pathology) / DNB (Pathology)
- **Head & Neck Cancer Surgery** — MS (ENT) / DNB (ENT)

## Admission

There is a **centralised admission process by MUHS, Nashik**. Contact: The Registrar,
Maharashtra University of Health Sciences, Vani–Dindori Road, Mhasrul, Nashik 422 004 —
[www.muhs.ac.in](https://www.muhs.ac.in).

> Note: The institution has also applied for fellowship courses in Medical Oncology and
> Onco-Anaesthesia.
