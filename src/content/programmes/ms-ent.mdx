## About the programme

The Sanjeevan Medical Foundation ENT Post Graduate Training Institute offers the
**MS (ENT) — Otorhinolaryngology** degree. The erstwhile **DLO (ENT)** diploma was
converted to **MS (ENT)** from the academic year 2020–21.

The programme is recognised by the **National Medical Commission (NMC / erstwhile MCI)**
vide MCI Letter No. 46(22)/2019-Med/MCI/178297 dated 21/02/2019, and is affiliated to the
**Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS), Nashik**.

## Seats

Three (3) MS (ENT) seats are recognised and filled each academic year through
**NEET PG / CET** by the Competent Authority. See the current
[Seat Matrix](/admissions#seat-matrix) for the year-wise position.

## Eligibility & selection

Candidates must hold an MBBS degree with a recognised registration and a valid NEET-PG
rank. Admissions are conducted through the centralised counselling of the Competent
Authority — there is no separate institutional entrance test.

## How to apply

Prospective candidates should participate in the **NEET-PG / State CET counselling** and
opt for this institute. For queries, use the [Admission Enquiry](/contact) form or contact
the admissions coordinators listed on the Contact page.
