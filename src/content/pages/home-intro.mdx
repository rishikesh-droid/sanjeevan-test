Established in **1984**, the **Sanjeevan Medical Foundation ENT Post Graduate Training
Institute** is a dedicated postgraduate institute for **ENT (Otorhinolaryngology)** at
Miraj, Maharashtra. Our MS (ENT) and Fellowship programmes are recognised by the National
Medical Commission and affiliated to the Maharashtra University of Health Sciences, Nashik —
combining academic rigour with hands-on surgical training across two well-equipped campuses.
