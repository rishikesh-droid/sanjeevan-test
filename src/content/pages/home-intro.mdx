title: Welcome to Sanjeevan PGI
description: Postgraduate ENT institute at Miraj, established 1984.
---

Established in **1984**, the **Sanjeevan Medical Foundation ENT Post Graduate Training
Institute** is a postgraduate institute for **ENT (Otorhinolaryngology)** at
Miraj, Maharashtra. Its MS (ENT) and Fellowship programmes are recognised by the National
Medical Commission and affiliated to the Maharashtra University of Health Sciences, Nashik.
