## Our History

The **Sanjeevan Medical Foundation ENT Post Graduate Training Institute**, Miraj has been
dedicated to postgraduate education and training in **ENT (Otorhinolaryngology)** for four
decades.

The ENT Department was initially recognised by the **National Board of Examinations,
Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, New Delhi** as a Postgraduate Training Institute
**since 1984**, for training PG students for the **Diplomate NB (Otorhinolaryngology)**.

From the academic year **1990–91**, the institute obtained affiliation from **Shivaji
University, Kolhapur** for training PG candidates for **M.S. (ENT)** and **D.L.O. (ENT)**,
with an intake capacity of MS (ENT) — 1 candidate per year and DLO (ENT) — 2 candidates
per year.

In **2010**, the institute was recognised by the **Medical Council of India, New Delhi**
(vide letter No. MCI-92[22]/2010-Med./33731 dated 12/10/2010) for training PG students for
MS (ENT) & DLO (ENT) under the **Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS),
Nashik**, with retrospective effect from the academic year 1991–92.

This recognition was **renewed in 2019** by the Medical Council of India (vide MCI Letter
No. 46(22)/2019-Med/MCI/178297 dated 21/02/2019). As per the new rules, from the academic
year **2020–21 the PG Diploma course (DLO) was converted into the PG Degree course, MS
(ENT)** — and the total intake capacity of the degree course was increased **from 1 to 3
seats per year**.

Alongside the ENT institute, the foundation's **Dr. D. K. Gosavi Memorial Shri
Siddhivinayak Ganapati Cancer Hospital, Miraj** offers MUHS-affiliated fellowships in
Onco-Pathology and Head & Neck Cancer Surgery.

## Mission

To provide rigorous, ethical and compassionate postgraduate training in ENT and allied
super-specialities — producing skilled clinicians and surgeons who serve their communities
with excellence.
